Why Website Costs Vary So Much in India

You've probably seen quotes ranging from ₹5,000 to ₹5,00,000 for "a website." That gap isn't random. Several things drive cost up or down:

  • Type of website — a portfolio is nothing like an ecommerce store
  • Who builds it — a freelancer on Fiverr vs. a professional agency
  • Custom design vs. template — original UI costs more but converts better
  • Features and integrations — payment gateways, booking systems, CRMs
  • Content creation — copywriting, photography, and SEO
  • Ongoing maintenance — hosting, updates, and security patches

Understanding these levers is the first step to getting a fair quote.

What's Actually Included in These Prices?

Basic Static Website (₹8,000 – ₹25,000)

This gets you a clean HTML/CSS/JS site with standard pages like Home, About, Services, Contact. No CMS, no database. Good for small businesses that just need an online presence. Think of a local bakery, a tutor's portfolio, or a small law firm.

What's typically not included at this price point: custom illustrations, animations, SEO copywriting, or ongoing maintenance.

Business / Corporate Website (₹25,000 – ₹80,000)

This is the sweet spot for most Indian SMEs. You get a responsive multi-page website with a custom design, basic SEO setup, a contact form, Google Maps integration, and a simple CMS so you can update content yourself. Suitable for coaching institutes, clinics, real estate agencies, and manufacturing firms.

Ecommerce Website (₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000)

Selling products online is a completely different beast. You need a product catalog, shopping cart, payment gateway integration (Razorpay, PayU, or Stripe), order management, and GST invoicing. The cost jumps because there's backend development, security hardening, and significantly more testing involved.

A Shopify-based store will sit at the lower end of this range. A fully custom-built solution using Laravel or Node.js will push toward the top.

Custom Web Application (₹1,00,000 – ₹10,00,000+)

This covers SaaS products, booking platforms, school ERPs, hospital management systems, inventory tools, and CRM dashboards. These aren't websites in the traditional sense — they're software that happens to run in a browser. Every feature is built from scratch, tested thoroughly, and maintained over time.

Hidden Costs Most People Forget

The development quote is just one part of the total investment. Here are costs that often catch businesses off guard:

  • Domain name: ₹700 – ₹1,500/year (depending on the TLD)
  • Web hosting: ₹3,000 – ₹30,000/year (shared to VPS)
  • SSL certificate: Free (Let's Encrypt) to ₹8,000/year (premium)
  • Content writing: ₹2,000 – ₹8,000 per page for professional SEO copy
  • Stock photography / custom photos: ₹5,000 – ₹50,000+
  • Annual maintenance: ₹8,000 – ₹60,000/year depending on complexity
  • Payment gateway charges: 2–3% per transaction (not a one-time fee)

How to Evaluate a Website Quote

Before you sign anything, ask your developer these questions:

  1. Is the design custom or a purchased template?
  2. Who owns the code once the project is complete?
  3. Is SEO setup included (meta tags, sitemap, schema)?
  4. How many revision rounds are included?
  5. What happens if I need changes after launch?
  6. Will the site be mobile-responsive and tested on real devices?
  7. Do you provide hosting, or is that separate?

A serious, professional studio will answer all of these without hesitation. Evasive answers on any of these points should raise a flag.

Common Mistakes When Budgeting for a Website

  • Going with the lowest quote — a ₹3,000 website almost always looks like a ₹3,000 website
  • Forgetting recurring costs — domain renewal, hosting, maintenance
  • Not budgeting for content — good copy and images are half the battle
  • Treating it as a one-time purchase — your website needs regular updates to stay secure and relevant
  • Skipping mobile testing — over 75% of Indian users browse on mobile

Expert Tips to Get More From Your Budget

  • Start with a focused 5-page site and expand as you grow — don't over-build on day one
  • Invest in good photography; nothing kills a well-designed site faster than blurry phone photos
  • Ask for a CMS so you can update content without calling the developer every time
  • Make sure Google Analytics and Search Console are set up before launch
  • Prioritise page speed — a 1-second delay reduces conversions by 7% according to Google data

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a basic website cost in India in 2026?

A basic 5-page static website from a professional agency costs between ₹8,000 and ₹25,000. Template-based freelancer work can be cheaper but quality varies significantly.

Is ₹10,000 enough to build a good website?

For a very simple static site with minimal pages, yes — but you won't get custom design, SEO setup, or ongoing support at that price. Consider it a starting floor, not an ideal budget.

How much does an ecommerce website cost in India?

Expect to pay ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 for a functional ecommerce site with payment integration, product management, and mobile optimisation.

What is the annual maintenance cost for a website?

Annual maintenance typically ranges from ₹8,000 to ₹60,000 depending on the type of site. This covers hosting, security updates, plugin/framework updates, and minor content changes.

Should I choose a freelancer or an agency for my website?

If it's a simple brochure site on a tight budget, a good freelancer works fine. For business-critical, complex, or ecommerce sites — an agency with a proper team and SLA offers far better reliability and results.

Summary

Website development cost in India in 2026 ranges from ₹5,000 for a basic landing page to ₹10,00,000+ for a full custom web application. The right investment depends on your goals, your audience, and how central your website is to your business.

The most important thing? Don't treat your website as an expense. Treat it as a sales and marketing asset — because that's exactly what a well-built one is.
